# 📊 E-commerce Sales Analysis | Excel, SQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my **Data Analyst Internship at Primeor Solutions**.

The project focuses on analysing an e-commerce sales dataset to understand business performance across different regions, markets, product categories and customer segments.

I worked with Excel, MySQL and Power BI to clean the data, perform analysis, identify business trends and build an interactive dashboard.

The main goal was to turn raw sales data into meaningful insights that can help understand business performance and support better decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, preparation, formulas, PivotTables and charts.
- **MySQL Workbench** – SQL queries, data analysis and business-related calculations.
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, slicers and data visualisation.

## 📂 Dataset Information

- Total Records: 51,271
- Total Columns: 21
- Data Period: 2011–2014

The dataset contains information about:
- Orders and shipping dates
- Customer details and segments
- Product categories and sub-categories
- Sales, quantity, discounts and profit
- Regions, markets and countries
- Shipping modes and shipping costs

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning – Excel

I started by exploring the dataset and preparing it for analysis.

The cleaning process included:

- Checking and removing duplicate records.
- Identifying and handling missing values.
- Correcting date formats.
- Removing unnecessary spaces.
- Standardising text values.
- Checking numerical columns.
- Reviewing negative profit values.

### 2. Data Analysis – Excel

I used Excel formulas and PivotTables to explore the data.

Some of the analysis included:

- Total sales and profit calculations.
- Average discount analysis.
- Top products by sales.
- Region-wise sales comparison.
- Category and segment analysis.
- Monthly sales trends.

### 3. SQL Analysis – MySQL

I imported the cleaned dataset into MySQL Workbench and performed analysis using SQL queries.

The analysis covered:

1. Top 10 profitable products.
2. Top 10 customers by sales.
3. Region-wise total sales.
4. Average profit by category.
5. Category with the highest average discount.
6. Orders with negative profit.
7. Monthly sales trends.
8. Market-wise revenue.
9. Top-performing product sub-categories.
10. Shipping mode usage.

SQL concepts used:

`SELECT`, `WHERE`, `GROUP BY`, `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT`, `SUM()`, `AVG()`, `COUNT()`, `DISTINCT` and date functions.

### 4. Interactive Dashboard – Power BI

I developed a three-page interactive dashboard to present the analysis visually.

**Page 1 – Executive Overview**

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Discount
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Category
- Orders by Shipping Mode

**Page 2 – Sales Analysis**

- Sales by Region
- Sales by Market
- Category Performance
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Interactive Year, Region and Category filters

**Page 3 – Product & Customer Insights**

- Total Products
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin
- Top Products by Sales
- Top Customers by Sales
- Profit by Sub-category
- Customer Segment Analysis

## 📈 Key Business Insights

Some of the important findings from the analysis:

- Total sales amounted to approximately **12.64 million**.
- Total profit was approximately **1.47 million**.
- APAC recorded the highest market-wise sales.
- Central was the highest sales-generating region.
- Technology contributed the highest total profit among the three product categories.
- Phones generated the highest sales among the product sub-categories.
- Copiers generated the highest profit among the sub-categories.
- Consumer customers contributed the highest sales among the three customer segments.
- Tables recorded a negative total profit, highlighting an area that may require further investigation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, some areas that could be explored further include:

- Reviewing the performance of regions with comparatively lower sales.
- Monitoring discount strategies and their impact on profitability.
- Giving attention to high-profit products and sub-categories.
- Investigating loss-making products and transactions.
- Understanding customer purchasing patterns.
- Monitoring monthly sales trends to support business planning.
